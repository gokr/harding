Richards Harding port

Reference: Chromium Octane, richards.js
Pinned commit: 570ad1ccfe86e3eecba0636c8f932ac08edec517
https://github.com/chromium/octane/blob/570ad1ccfe86e3eecba0636c8f932ac08edec517/richards.js

The reference states it implements Martin Richards' original BCPL benchmark.
This Harding port retains its six tasks, priorities, packet layout, linked-list
queues, 1,000 idle iterations and expected counters (2,322 queues / 928 holds).
No Sun/Wolczko or AWFY source is incorporated.
